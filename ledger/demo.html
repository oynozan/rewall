Giving a server access to your secrets, without giving it your secrets

A program running somewhere else wants to use credentials you own. Your Ledger decides whether it is allowed in. Rewall carries the permission to it, addressed to a name, so nothing secret is ever copied onto that machine.

This is a real Ledger app running in Ledger's own emulator, so you can see the screen. Press right to move and both to approve.

1. Turn on the Ledger

Boots the device so you can see its screen and press its buttons.

2. A server asks to join

It creates its own key and asks for permission. Your Ledger will show the request, and nothing happens until you approve it on the device.

3. Unplug the Ledger

Proves what comes next needs no device at all.

4. The server does its work

With no device anywhere, it reads its permission off the blockchain and uses the secret.
