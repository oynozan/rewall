# Quiet key

`quiet-key.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for the dashboard’s welcome and guidance states.

Prompt: A small metal key resting diagonally across a folded paper envelope. Monochrome pixel art with ordered dither stippling, white and charcoal only, centered on a flat dark charcoal background with generous empty space. No text, logo, watermark, gradients, glow, soft shadow, color, or extra objects.

The original generated raster is preserved. Next.js produces the display sizes.
