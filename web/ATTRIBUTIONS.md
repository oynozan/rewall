# Dashboard assets

- Icons are unmodified white SVGs from [Arcticons](https://github.com/Arcticons-Team/Arcticons/tree/main/icons/white), distributed under [GPL-3.0](public/licenses/arcticons.txt).
- `FadeIn` and `FadeDots` adapt the corresponding [Amicro](https://github.com/Subhan-code/Amicro--Micro-transitions-/tree/main/registry/ui) components. Changes use `motion/react`, respect reduced motion, and apply the dashboard’s sizes. The [MIT license](public/licenses/amicro.txt) is included.
- Manrope, Lexend Deca, and Ubuntu Mono are loaded through `next/font/google` and self-hosted by Next.js.
- The key illustration was generated with the built-in image generation tool. Its [prompt and asset notes](public/illustrations/asset-notes.md) are included.
- The Rewall wordmark and symbol use the supplied project SVGs. The two supplied wordmark drawings alternate on hover.
