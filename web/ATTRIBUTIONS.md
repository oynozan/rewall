# Dashboard assets

- Icons are unmodified white SVGs from [Arcticons](https://github.com/Arcticons-Team/Arcticons/tree/main/icons/white), distributed under [GPL-3.0](public/licenses/arcticons.txt).
- `FadeIn`, `SidebarFade`, `SidebarSection`, and `FadeDots` adapt the corresponding [Amicro](https://github.com/Subhan-code/Amicro--Micro-transitions-/tree/main/registry/ui) components. Changes use `motion/react`, respect reduced motion, and apply the dashboard’s sizes. The [MIT license](public/licenses/amicro.txt) is included.
- Arcticons is an app icon set and has no entry for close, refresh, copy, or chevron controls. Those controls are drawn in `Glyph`, on the same 48 unit grid with the same round stroke, and are original to this repo.
- Manrope, Lexend Deca, Akt, and Ubuntu Mono are loaded through `next/font/google` and self-hosted by Next.js.
- The ENS, Ethereum and Chainlink marks in the landing hero come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), released under [CC0 1.0](public/licenses/simple-icons.txt). The Ledger mark is the brand owner’s own artwork from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ledger-logo-long.svg), released under [CC BY-SA 4.0](public/licenses/ledger-logo.txt). Ethereum and Ledger publish monochrome marks, so their hover uses the white treatment their own guidelines give for a dark ground. Unmodified originals are in `public/brands`, and the page renders each path with `currentColor` so a hover restores the brand colour. All four are used to name real integrations, not to imply endorsement.
- The Rewall wordmark and symbol use the supplied project SVGs. The two supplied wordmark drawings alternate on hover.
- The light rays behind the clients section are the `LightRays` background from [react-bits](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits), vendored rather than installed through its CLI, and adapted with a client directive, a reduced-motion guard and a grayscale palette. It renders through [ogl](https://github.com/oframe/ogl), which is public domain. The [react-bits licence](public/licenses/react-bits.txt) is included.
- The flow beams use the `AnimatedBeam` component from the [uibeats registry](https://uibeats.com/r/animated-beam.json), installed by hand at `src/components/ui/animated-beam.tsx` rather than through the shadcn CLI, which would have scaffolded a `components.json` and a `cn` helper this project does not use. It needs only `motion`, which was already a dependency. Local changes are grayscale colours at the call sites and four additions the flows need: right angle routing, endpoints that land on the elements’ facing edges and on whole pixels so a rail stays sharp, a `speed` in pixels per second so beams of different lengths travel together, and a pulse drawn as a dash riding the path rather than a gradient swept across the box, because a linear gradient is a straight band and lights both legs of a corner at once instead of turning it.
- The waves under the How it works section are the `GradientWaves` background from [react-bits](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits), vendored the same way as the light rays and adapted with a client directive, a reduced-motion guard that renders one still frame, a pointer transparent root and a grayscale palette. It renders through the same [ogl](https://github.com/oframe/ogl) dependency, and the [react-bits licence](public/licenses/react-bits.txt) already included covers it.
